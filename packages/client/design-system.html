Spacing & Radius

Layout runs on flex utilities (.stack/.row/.spread) with ad-hoc gaps rather than a strict numeric scale — the gaps in real use cluster at these steps.

--radius = 6px, used on every card, button, input and chip.

Motion

Hard and physical: presses translate INTO the drop-shadow instead of easing. --ease-snap = cubic-bezier(.2,1.4,.4,1). Click/press each card below to feel the real transition. Everything collapses to ~0 under prefers-reduced-motion.

Buttons

Ink border, hard shadow. :active translates 4px,4px into the shadow. Ghost is dashed with no shadow.

Inputs

Inset shadow at rest; cyan focus outline (tab into the field or click it).
